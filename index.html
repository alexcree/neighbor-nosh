    straight talk

    Safety, honestly

    We don't vet cooks

    Neighbor Nosh doesn't run background checks or inspect kitchens. Permits and food safety are each cook's responsibility — ask questions, as you would with any classified ad.

    It's between you two

    Once you connect, the arrangement — food, price, pickup or delivery — is yours together. Start small and build trust over a few meals.

    You can report a cook

    If something goes wrong, report a cook to Neighbor Nosh and their email can be blocked from the board.

    Does Neighbor Nosh cost anything?

    No. Posting and applying are free, and there's no fee when you match. You pay your cook directly — cash, Venmo, whatever you both prefer. Neighbor Nosh never touches the money.

    Who sees my phone number?

    Nobody from a public post. If you're a cook, your phone and email appear only inside your application, which only the person who posted can see.

    Where is this launching?

    First in the Coachella Valley — with cooks and meal-seekers joining at the same time, so the board is useful from day one.
